### Task
Write a short first-person narrative in which an anonymous person expresses a
strongly held opinion about the Objective Anchor, arguing for the side
indicated by the Focal Evidence.

### Voice
The narrator is an anonymous, opinionated commenter. They are emotionally
engaged and personally invested in the issue, but they reveal NOTHING about
who they are.

### Hard Constraints --- the narrative MUST NOT reveal or imply the narrator's identity
1. No identity claims. Never write phrases such as "as a conservative",
   "speaking as someone who", "people like me", "in my community",
   "for folks like us", or any statement of group membership.
2. No demographic or group markers. Do not reference religion or religious
   practice, occupation or profession, age or generation, geography or region,
   race or ethnicity, family role, income bracket, or party/candidate
   affiliation --- not even indirectly (e.g., "rural folks", "us churchgoers",
   "at my age", "small-business owners like me", "this administration").
3. No value-laden self-labels. Do not describe yourself with terms that signal
   a political type (e.g., "I'm a patriot", "I'm progressive", "as a taxpayer
   who believes in hard work").
4. Personal stake is allowed only in generic, identity-neutral terms. You may
   say the issue affects you, worries you, or that you have followed it
   closely; you may NOT say who you are or which group you belong to.

### Content Constraints
5. Focus on the Evidence: build the opinion around the Focal Evidence drawn
   from the Objective Anchor. Treat it as the main reason the narrator holds
   their view.
6. First-person and opinionated: take a clear, one-sided stance. Be
   emotionally engaged, not balanced.
7. Argue only from the anchor: do not introduce statistics, events, or facts
   not present in the Objective Anchor.
8. Leading question: end with a question to the reader asking whether they
   agree with your conclusion.
9. Length: approximately 200 words.

### Input Data
- Objective Anchor: {objective_anchor}
- Focal Evidence (the side to argue for): {mapping_A_or_B}

### Output
Return only the narrative text. No preamble, no headings, no quotation marks
around the whole text.
