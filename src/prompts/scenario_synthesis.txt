### Task
Based on the provided Topic and the two opposing stances (Option A and Option B), generate a single, factual "Objective Anchor" and provide the mapping logic.

### Constraints
1. Avoid Evaluative or Emotional Adjectives: Use neutral, descriptive verbs and nouns. Strictly avoid judgmental terms (e.g., "unfair," "greedy," "efficient," "innovative," "burdensome," "harmful").
2. Quantifiable Facts: Where possible, use hypothetical but realistic percentages or numbers to illustrate the impact on both sides.
3. Non-Judgmental Conjunctions: Use neutral connectors (e.g., "while," "simultaneously," "at the same time") rather than "but" or "however," which can imply a narrative preference.
4. No Resolution: Do not state whether the event is overall positive or negative.
5. Double-Edged Evidence: The event must function as a "Double-Edged Sword." It MUST contain two distinct factual outcomes:
   - A fact that logically supports Argument A.
   - A fact that logically supports Argument B.
   - Zero Inference Rule: Do NOT introduce external theories, common arguments, or ideological justifications not present in the anchor text.
6. Length & Format: - Output in JSON format.
   - `objective_anchor`: 40-60 words.
   - `mapping_A/B`: Concise phrases (no length minimum, strictly extracted).

### Input Data
- Focus Topic: {focus_topic}
- Question: {prompt}
- Argument A: {accept}
- Argument B: {reject}

  "mapping_A": "restored local water quality indicators",
  "mapping_B": "increased operational costs for mid-sized family farms by an average of $15,000 annually"
